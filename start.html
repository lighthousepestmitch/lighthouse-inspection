How to use these tools

Your iPad and every job. Tap the LPC logo to go back to Home.

Set up your iPad (once)

  1. Point the Camera at the code on your card. Tap the link that pops up. It opens in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open the new icon on your Home Screen. You will see five tools.
  4. Tap Job Calendar. Log in once with the email and password Mitch gave you. Same login for every tool.
  5. While you have signal, open each of the five tools once. After that they open with no signal. Home says Opens offline at the bottom when the main tools are saved.

To get back to Home from any tool, tap the LPC logo at the top.

Every job

  1. Before you leave. Open Job Calendar and tap the job. Check the address and the customer notes. Check the ute is stocked (Field Guides, Vehicle check).
  2. On arrival. Park so you don't block driveways. PPE on before you go in. Say hello and tell them your name. Ask where the problem spots are.
  3. Termite inspection. On the job, tap Start inspection report. Capture opens with the job's address and client filled in. For each part tap Clear, N/A, Finding or Undet. A finding needs a note. Add photos as you go. When you are done tap Finish, read it through, tap Send to Mitch and wait for Sent.
  4. Any other treatment. On the job, tap Service record. Pick what you did and the products you used, then tap Save. Take before and after photos.
  5. Last thing. Tap Mark done on the job. Tell Mitch about any follow-up or anything unusual.
  6. Not sure? Field Guides has the steps and checklists. A spill or a chemical question? SDS Sheets.

No signal?

If you opened each tool once with signal, they still open. Job Calendar shows the jobs it saved last time (you can look, not change). Capture keeps your job on the iPad.

Logging in and sending need signal. If a send fails, try again when you have signal, and don't clear the job until Mitch says it arrived.
